# Power measurements

Choose scenarios from the task's actual operating modes and power questions. For each, explain in
verification-plan.md the workload, clocks and relevant state, measurement interval/statistic,
budget applicability and required verification capabilities. Initialization may be included or
excluded according to the question; identify which. Define enough to distinguish the measurements
without implementing the future experiment here.

Use power-scenarios.json only to list their identifiers. There is no mandatory scenario roster,
throughput, low-power mode, or sequence binding. A report-only task still needs a meaningful
measurement. Power-analysis organizes the executable workload, checks and capture.
